RINGLOCK

One tap. Don't miss.

What it is

A pointer sweeps around a ring. Tap when it crosses the marked slice. Hit the middle and the slice stays wide; miss the middle and it narrows. Every round has its own layout, and the whole game works offline.

Support

Something broken, an idea, or a name on the leaderboard that should not be there — write to us and you will get a reply.

ringlockgame@gmail.com

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection? No. The game is fully playable offline. Only the global leaderboard needs a connection, and it fails quietly when there isn't one.
Why is my score not on the leaderboard? Scores are only sent once you set a display name in the lobby, and only when you beat your own best. If you have no name set, nothing is sent.
How do I change my name? Tap your name in the lobby. Your existing leaderboard entry updates to match; it does not create a second one.
How do I get my score removed? Email us and it will be deleted.
I saw an offensive name. Email us. Names are filtered automatically, but a filter is not a guarantee, so please do report what gets through.

Privacy

Ringlock shows no ads and contains no tracking. The only thing it ever sends is the display name you choose and your score, and only once you have chosen a name. Read the full privacy policy.
